Explainable discovery

The result is a decision, not just a title match

  1. Reduce: deterministic text matching and explicit facets create a reviewable candidate set.
  2. Differentiate: population, measure, unit, geography, time, frequency, version and method are compared.
  3. Verify: QMI, methodology, provenance, revisions and standards evidence stay linked to the source.
  4. Configure: required dimensions remain explicit until a read-only MCP plan is valid.
